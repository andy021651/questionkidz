Question Kidz App – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-05

What this app is

Question Kidz App is a private desktop tool used only by the host of the Question Kidz podcast (YouTube channel @QuestionKidz) to plan episodes, upload the show's own videos to its own channel, and read that channel's statistics. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • YouTube Data API: the channel's own video list, titles and public statistics (views, likes, comments); uploading the channel's own videos and thumbnails.
  • YouTube Analytics API: the channel's own views, watch time, shares and subscribers gained per video.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to show the channel's own numbers inside the app and to publish the channel's own videos. The OAuth refresh token is stored only in the macOS Keychain on the host's computer. Statistics are stored only on that computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or sent to any server other than Google's APIs.

Retention and deletion

Stored data can be deleted at any time by removing the app's data folder and Keychain entries. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
