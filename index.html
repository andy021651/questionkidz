Question Kidz App

Desktop tool for the Question Kidz podcast

About

Question Kidz is an independent podcast that talks with creators across art forms. Episodes are published on SoundOn and on the YouTube channel @QuestionKidz.

Question Kidz App is the host's private desktop tool for planning episodes, uploading the show's own videos to its own YouTube channel, and reading that channel's statistics. It uses YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public.
